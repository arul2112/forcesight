Questions

What Finance Teams Ask Before the First Call.What Teams Ask Before the First Call.

Is this a reconciliation tool or a P&L tool?

Both, because for a brand selling through marketplaces they are the same question. Reconciliation determines what your revenue and deductions actually were. Until that is settled, the P&L is an estimate. ForceSight covers profit, settlements, inventory and advertising as inputs to one contribution figure and one cash position.

Which marketplaces and platforms do you cover?

Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Ajio, Nykaa, Snapdeal, and the quick-commerce platforms Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart, alongside your own website and the ad platforms running against them. A single-channel tool cannot close your month, because the deductions sit across all of them.

Does an AI model calculate any of my numbers?

No. Figures are computed by a deterministic reconciliation engine against your own records. The language layer explains, ranks and proposes from a governed catalogue. It is never permitted to emit a rupee value.

How do you know a fix actually worked?

Every proposed course of action carries one of three verification classes before you choose it: checked by your data, proof you attach, or you confirm and we state plainly that we cannot track it. Only the first is counted automatically against confirmed impact.

Do you replace our accounting system or our BI stack?

No. Your books stay where they are. ForceSight sits between the platform data and the close, producing the reconciled position that your accounting system and your board pack both depend on.

What do you need to start?

Read access to your marketplace seller accounts, ad accounts and order data. Most brands see a first brief within days rather than weeks, because ForceSight reads the files and reports you already have rather than requiring a data warehouse project first.
